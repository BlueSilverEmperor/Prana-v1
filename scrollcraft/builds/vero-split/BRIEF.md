## Grammar: split stage
Two sides the whole way down: "What you see" and "What runs". Divider is the chrome (labels, progress, the action).
Close is the collapse: divider travels to the left edge, the "What runs" side takes the full width.

## Signature move
The divider is a chain of SHA-256-hashed glass blocks computed in the page. One block is mined per act; at the peak the
live check's transaction block (real `raw_tx_hash` from `/v1/simulate`) descends, fails to link and is ejected.
