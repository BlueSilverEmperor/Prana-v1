# Vero Console: brief (v4)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation** ("make it awesome", "more alive and animations").
The surface is an Operate-mode dashboard, not a scroll story, so scroll-craft supplies principles here
(one signature move, motion with purpose, feeling curve, real numbers only), not its act engine.

**User direction (verbatim):** "metallic alive black as primary colour", "electric red as secondary",
"make the navigation bar as the notch above at top", "frosted glass-translucent frosted navigation bar with lil
animations and cooler".

## Feeling curve
| Moment | Feeling | Caused by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Load | Arrival | The glass notch drops in from the screen edge; tiles rise in sequence |
| Reading the numbers | Trust | Real benchmark figures with intervals, charts draw once |
| Moving around | Control | A red pill slides to the current section; a light follows the pointer |
| Running a check (peak) | Certainty | The verdict drops out of the notch as an island: "Blocked · risk 95" |

**Peak:** "You run a transaction and the verdict drops out of the notch at the top, like a phone alert."
**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the console where the verdict drops out of the notch the moment you check a transaction.

## Signature move
The island: each check the visitor runs drops a frosted verdict pill out of the notch for 2.8 s (not the boot check).

## Motion inventory
- Notch: entrance drop (first load only), 9 s sheen, red scroll-progress rim, sliding clip-path pill, hover layer.
- Tiles: pointer spotlight (registered custom properties, no layout), staggered entrance.
- Verdict: badge pop and staggered reasons (WAAPI, interruptible).
- Ambient: two drifting lights and a dot lattice, transform-only.
- Reduced motion: every loop and slide is off; opacity changes stay.
